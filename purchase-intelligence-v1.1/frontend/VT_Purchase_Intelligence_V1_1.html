Data quality

🔎
Smart search: try 263640557, 26*36*40*55*7, tbush dm ltb 4 — even reversed digits work as a last resort.
MOQ — Minimum Order Quantity: the typical order size you place (from history). Confirm what the supplier actually demands. EOQ — Economic Order Quantity: the most cost-efficient quantity per order, balancing ordering cost vs holding cost. Safety Stock — buffer quantity to keep so you don't run out when demand jumps or supply is late. Reorder Point — when stock falls to this level, place the next order (covers lead-time demand + safety stock). Yearly Demand — implied from your purchase outflow over the data period (register has no consumption data). Lead time — days from order to delivery. Set a default below, or a per-supplier value using the supplier filter.
Demand is implied from purchase outflow (no consumption data in the register). EOQ = √(2DS/H); safety stock uses monthly demand variability over lead time. Treat suggestions as starting points and confirm with the team.
