Approve/calibrate ABC, XYZ, FSN, demand pattern, customer concentration, exceptional-demand detection, planning lead time, fill rate and OTIF.

## Stage 3 — Replenishment policy
Only after reliable stock and supply semantics: safety stock, reorder point, coverage/target stock and policy ownership.

## Stage 4 — Technical architecture
Choose persistence, interfaces, synchronization, audit, migration and security only after business semantics are stable.

## Stage 5 — Incremental implementation
Implement through explicit work items/tests. Pre-V2 scaffolding does not count as completed V2 implementation.

## Stage 6 — Migration and validation
Coexist with V1 where required; remove fallbacks only after explicit validation/approval.
